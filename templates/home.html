Get to know the wrinkle levels before scanning your skin:

Mild Wrinkles

Fine lines that appear during facial expressions. Often reversible with hydration and protection.

Moderate Wrinkles

Visible at rest, not just during expressions. Caused by aging and sun exposure. Treatable with active care.

Severe Wrinkles

Deep lines visible at all times. More common in mature skin. Requires consistent anti-aging routines.
